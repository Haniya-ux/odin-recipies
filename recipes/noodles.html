Noodles Recipe

Description

Perfect treat for movie night on a cozy winter evening

Ingredients

  • 1 packet Noodles
  • 1 packet Noodles masaala
  • 3 tbsp Vinegar
  • 2 tbsp Soy sauce
  • 1 cup water
  • Chopped spring onions
  • 1 egg

Steps

  1. Pour water in a pan and add Noodles
  2. Boil it then add vinegar, soy sauce and noodles masaala
  3. In another pan, boil the egg.
  4. Turn off heat and add noodles to a bowl.
  5. serve with spring onions and boiled egg.
