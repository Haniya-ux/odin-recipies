Lasagna Recipe

Description

Mouth watering cheesy crispy chicken lasagna.

Ingredients

  • Cheese
  • Chicken
  • Pasta
  • Salt
  • Red chilli
  • Black Pepper

Steps

  1. cut the chicken into small pieces
  2. marinate it.
  3. boil the pasta then drain it.
  4. heat the pan and add oil.
  5. add chicken and spices to the pan.
  6. add some cheese
  7. add pasta.
  8. add more cheese then lower the flame.
  9. cook for 4 minutes
  10. serve hot.
