Fruit Bowl Recipe

Description

The perfect healthy and delicious breakfast Bowl.

Ingredients

  • greek yoghurt
  • fruits of your liking
  • oats
  • nuts

Steps

  1. Take your favorite bowl.
  2. Put two scoops of greek yoghurt.
  3. cut fruit into small pieces.
  4. Add fruit to the bowl.
  5. For Toppings, add oats and nuts.
  6. Enjoy!
